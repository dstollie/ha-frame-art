# Series (playlists) — 15 Nov 2026

Goal: playlists of works that rotate on one or more Frames, user-friendly from a work, with HA entities and MCP. Decision record: knowledge/codebase/06-adr-series-playback.md.

## Done

1. **frame-tv:** `contentIds()` (`get_content_list`, My Photos) + FakeFrame support and error case.
2. **Server:** `series.ts` (tables `series`, `series_items`; CRUD, order, per-work duration), `player.ts` (playback per TV in `meta`, timer, shuffle per round, follows edits, never wakes a TV after the first step, pause/resume/next/stop, hold on manual show), upload re-use in `jobs.ts` (`uploads.mode`, `showExisting`), cleanup protects current + next.
3. **API:** `/series` CRUD, `/series/:id/items`, `/order`, `/play`, `/tvs/:tv/playback`; artwork detail lists its series; TV state carries `playback`.
4. **MQTT:** per TV `select …_serie` + buttons next/pause/stop; per series a sensor; republished on reconnect.
5. **MCP:** `list_series`, `get_series`, `create_series`, `update_series`, `delete_series`, `play_series`, `control_playback`.
6. **Panel:** on a work *Add to series* / *New series* (inline); *Series* tab with now playing; `/series/:id` with drag/arrow reorder, durations, shuffle, play per TV, now/next and controls; *Series from these results* (Explore) and *from this album*.
7. **Tests:** `test/series.test.ts` (16), frame-tv content list (2), Playwright: work → new series → second work → play → edit → control → stop → delete; album and search; phone layout.
